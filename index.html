01 / ABOUT

Experience across the full system.

Hands-on engineering experience. Architectural thinking. A focus on measurable operational outcomes.

Paul Tupciauskas is an independent intelligent systems architect and consultant with decades of experience spanning industrial automation, manufacturing systems, enterprise software, data integration, optimization, and advanced intelligent applications.

His work bridges operational technology (OT), information technology (IT), and artificial intelligence—connecting plant-floor systems, enterprise platforms, people, and decision processes.

Experience includes manufacturing execution systems (MES), SCADA, industrial connectivity, production analytics, Unified Namespace (UNS) architectures, cloud integration, custom software development, and optimization.

Current interests extend these foundations into agentic AI, multi-agent orchestration, adaptive workflows, decision intelligence, and experimental cognitive architectures.
